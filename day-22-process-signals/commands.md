# Commands Practiced on Day 22

1. `kill` - Send signals (default SIGTERM) to process IDs.
2. `kill -9` - Force termination via SIGKILL.
3. `kill -HUP` - Send reload/hangup signal.
4. `killall` - Kill processes by exact name.
5. `pkill` - Signal processes matching name patterns.
6. `fg` - Bring background job to foreground.
7. `bg` - Resume suspended job in background.
8. `Ctrl+Z` - Suspend foreground task.
9. `nohup` - Run commands immune to hangups.
10. `disown` - Detach jobs from active shell control.
